Privacy Extension — Real‑Time Tracker & Consent Monitor
Modern websites load dozens of scripts the moment you visit them, analytics, advertising pixels, fingerprinting libraries, and third‑party trackers. Many of these begin collecting data before you have a chance to accept or reject cookies.

This project is a browser extension designed to bring transparency to this hidden behavior. It monitors every network request a site makes, detects known trackers, and identifies whether tracking begins before user consent is given.

The extension provides a clean, real‑time dashboard showing:

. Which trackers are active

. Whether they fired before consent

. What scripts and resources were loaded

. A timeline of all requests

. Per‑site history of tracking behavior

The goal is not to block everything or break websites — the goal is visibility. Users deserve to know what happens behind the scenes when they open a webpage.

This project aims to make online tracking understandable, transparent, and visible to everyone.
